> This document defines how Aurex should behave while pursuing its mission. It does not grant operational, regulatory, utility, or system-operator authority.

## 1. Role of This Document

Values describe the qualities Aurex seeks to embody. Principles are decision rules that should remain valid when commercial pressure, technical convenience, politics, funding, growth, or ambition pull in another direction.

The [Vision, Mission & Purpose](VISION-MISSION-PURPOSE.md) document defines why Aurex exists and what it seeks to do. This document defines the standards that should guide how it does that work.

## 2. Core Values

### Public Value

Aurex works on real energy-system problems and seeks measurable benefit for people, communities, enterprises, public services, and the wider economy.

### Engineering Integrity

Aurex distinguishes what is proven from what is assumed, proposed, simulated, or hoped for. Technical possibility is not the same as safety, legal permission, economic justification, or operational readiness.

### Openness

Aurex prefers open standards, transparent interfaces, inspectable architectures, portable data, and collaborative development over unnecessary proprietary dependence.

### Interoperability

Aurex designs for diverse vendors, devices, institutions, protocols, and long equipment lifecycles. Systems should be usable, replaceable, and governable across boundaries.

### Safety and Resilience

Aurex prioritizes human safety, equipment protection, cybersecurity, reliability, legitimate authority, and continuity of critical services before optimization or commercial value.

### Stewardship

Aurex treats infrastructure, knowledge, data, and partnerships as responsibilities requiring maintenance, documentation, review, improvement, and responsible retirement.

### African Capability

Aurex uses global knowledge and technology in ways that strengthen African engineering, research, institutional, operational, and technological capability over time.

## 3. Decision Principles

### 1. Evidence Before Scale

Aurex scales claims and systems only after evidence, governance, cybersecurity maturity, institutional trust, and appropriate authority support the next stage.

```text
Research -> Facility -> Multi-site -> Regional -> National
```

### 2. Safety Before Optimization

No efficiency, automation, market, or financial objective overrides safety, security, reliability, resilience, or critical-service continuity. Systems should fail in controlled, traceable, and recoverable ways where practical.

### 3. Authority Must Be Explicit

Technical capability does not create asset ownership, control authority, regulatory authority, or market authority. Control actions and consequential recommendations require documented responsibility and lawful authorization.

### 4. Coordinate Globally, Control Locally

Higher system levels may coordinate visibility, intent, and flexibility, while safety-critical control remains as close as practical to the equipment and its legitimate operator.

### 5. Build From Real Operating Units

Aurex should begin with practical, measurable units such as facilities, where energy supply, loads, metering, ownership, constraints, and user outcomes can be understood together.

### 6. Design for Interoperability and Failure

Systems should expose documented interfaces, use meaningful data models, support authentication and replacement, and remain safe when connectivity, cloud services, sensors, communications, or central platforms fail.

### 7. Purposeful and Secure Data

Collect only what has a defined purpose. Data must have clear ownership or stewardship, access rules, retention, protection, and accountability. Cybersecurity is part of infrastructure and safety from the architecture stage.

### 8. Human Accountability for AI

AI may support forecasting, detection, optimization, maintenance, planning, and decision-making. Unless specifically authorized, validated, monitored, and governed, AI remains advisory and accountable human authority remains in place.

### 9. Modernize Responsibly

Aurex should strengthen existing infrastructure where safe and economically justified before assuming replacement is necessary. Technology must fit real operating conditions, including unreliable connectivity, constrained budgets, diverse vendors, and long equipment lifecycles.

### 10. Truth, Traceability, and Learning

Important decisions should record the question, evidence, options, decision, authority, rationale, and result. Aurex must be willing to report when a solution does not work, economics are unfavorable, regulation is missing, or scaling is premature.

The practical application of these principles is developed in the [Code of Conduct](../../CODE-OF-CONDUCT.md), [Governance Framework](GOVERNANCE-FRAMEWORK.md), [Organization Structure & Decision Rights](ORGANIZATION-STRUCTURE-AND-DECISION-RIGHTS.md), and [Risk Management Framework](../03-operations-and-development/RISK-MANAGEMENT-FRAMEWORK.md).

## 4. Institutional Commitments

Aurex will:

- Choose open standards over unnecessary proprietary dependence.
- Prioritize safety, security, and legitimate authority.
- Separate problems, requirements, evidence, and solutions.
- Design for interoperability and African operating realities.
- Protect data and document important decisions.
- Strengthen local capability through collaboration.
- Scale only when evidence supports scaling.
- Remain transparent about what has and has not been proven.

These commitments are implemented through the [Open-Source & Licensing Framework](../02-technical-governance/OPEN-SOURCE-AND-LICENSING-FRAMEWORK.md), [Security & Responsible Technology Policy](../02-technical-governance/SECURITY-AND-RESPONSIBLE-TECHNOLOGY-POLICY.md), [Ethics, Sovereignty & Public-Interest Framework](../02-technical-governance/ETHICS-SOVEREIGNTY-AND-PUBLIC-INTEREST-FRAMEWORK.md), and [Contributing Framework](../02-technical-governance/CONTRIBUTING-FRAMEWORK.md).

## 5. Founder-to-Institution Principle

Aurex begins founder-led but should mature beyond personal dependence:

```text
Founder intent -> Documented knowledge -> Defined roles -> Defined authority -> Repeatable institution
```

The founder should protect purpose, establish early standards, document assumptions, invite review, and prepare the institution for continuity through governance and defined decision rights.

## 6. Decision Test

For significant decisions, Aurex should ask:

1. Does this advance the mission and create public value?
2. Is it technically credible, safe, secure, and appropriate to current maturity?
3. Is authority clear and documented?
4. Does it support interoperability and avoid unnecessary dependency?
5. Is the evidence sufficient for the proposed scale?
6. Are impacts on affected people, institutions, and public interests understood?

Decisions affecting safety-critical infrastructure, regulated operations, personal or operational data, or institutional authority require heightened review.

## Public-Facing Values

Aurex can express these principles publicly as:

> **Open. Responsible. Evidence-driven. Resilient. Collaborative. Stewarding.**

These short values are an external summary, not a replacement for the institutional principles or their downstream controls.
