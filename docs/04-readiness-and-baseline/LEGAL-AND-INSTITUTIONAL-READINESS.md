# Task 20 - Legal & Institutional Readiness
## Aurex Digital Solutions

**Version:** 1.0
**Status:** Foundational Draft / Draft v1.0
**Institution:** Aurex Digital Solutions
**Phase:** Legalization
**Jurisdictional starting point:** Kenya
**Scope:** Legal formation, corporate governance, regulatory boundaries, contracts, IP, data, cybersecurity, energy-sector authorization, employment, safety, procurement and institutional compliance
**Navigation:** [Previous: Programme Governance Framework](../03-operations-and-development/PROGRAMME-GOVERNANCE-FRAMEWORK.md) | [Home: Documentation Index](../../README.md) | [Next: Organization Documentation Baseline v1.0](ORGANIZATION-DOCUMENTATION-BASELINE-V1.0.md)

Task 19 established how Aurex governs programmes.

Task 20 establishes the boundary between **Aurex's internal institutional architecture and the external legal authority required to operate in the real world.**

> **Internal governance can define how Aurex intends to act. Only legitimate law, contracts and external authority determine what Aurex is legally permitted to do.**

This framework is an institutional readiness document, **not legal advice**. Material incorporation, tax, employment, energy-regulatory, contractual and infrastructure-control decisions should be verified against current Kenyan requirements and, where appropriate, qualified professional advice.

---

## 1. Purpose

Tasks 1-19 established Aurex's internal architecture.

Those documents can define:

- purpose;
- governance;
- decision rights;
- technical principles;
- risk;
- operations;
- programmes.

They cannot independently create:

- legal personality;
- contractual capacity;
- regulatory licenses;
- tax status;
- electrical-work authorization;
- utility authority;
- asset ownership;
- public procurement rights;
- permission to process all forms of data;
- permission to control third-party energy infrastructure.

Task 20 determines what must exist before Aurex moves from **institution under formation** toward **formal operating institution**.

---

## 2. Legal Readiness Doctrine

Aurex adopts:

```text
INTERNAL AUTHORITY
      ≠
LEGAL AUTHORITY
      ≠
REGULATORY AUTHORITY
      ≠
CONTRACTUAL AUTHORITY
      ≠
ASSET-OWNER AUTHORITY
      ≠
SYSTEM-OPERATION AUTHORITY
```

And:

> **Technical capability never creates legal permission by itself.**

## 3. Legalization Is a Gate

Legalization should not be treated as administrative paperwork performed after engineering.

It is part of infrastructure readiness.

```text
INSTITUTIONAL DESIGN
        ↓
LEGAL FORM
        ↓
RIGHTS + OBLIGATIONS
        ↓
REGULATORY MAPPING
        ↓
OPERATIONAL AUTHORITY
        ↓
CONTROLLED EXECUTION
```

## 27. Data Protection

Kenya's Data Protection Act regulates processing of personal data and establishes rights of data subjects and obligations of data controllers and processors. The current legal position must be verified from official sources and appropriate professional advice before consequential action.

Aurex must therefore determine whether each activity involves:

```text
PERSONAL DATA?
      ↓
WHY PROCESS IT?
      ↓
WHAT ROLE DOES AUREX HAVE?
      ↓
WHAT LAWFUL BASIS / REQUIREMENT APPLIES?
      ↓
WHAT RIGHTS APPLY?
      ↓
HOW IS IT PROTECTED?
```

---

## 28. Energy Data Is Not Automatically Personal Data

Not all energy telemetry is personal data.

But energy data may become personal or sensitive in context when it can relate to identifiable people, occupancy, behavior or other protected information.

Classification should depend on actual data and use.

## 36. Energy-Sector Regulatory Boundary

This is one of Task 20's most important areas.

Kenyan energy law may require licensing for specified electricity undertakings, including generation, import/export, transmission, distribution and retail supply, subject to statutory exceptions and current regulatory interpretation. The current legal position must be verified from official energy-sector sources and qualified advice before consequential action.

Aurex must therefore determine:

> **Which proposed Aurex activities are merely software/research/integration, and which could cross into regulated electricity activities?**

## 79. Legal Risk

Legal risk should be integrated into Task 13's risk-management system.

Examples include:

- operating without required authorization;
- signing unenforceable or unauthorized agreements;
- unclear IP ownership;
- data-protection noncompliance;
- unauthorized system access;
- unsafe field activity;
- employment misclassification;
- tax noncompliance;
- false public claims;
- procurement disqualification;
- uninsured liability;
- regulatory breach.

Legal risk is institutional risk.

## 80. No-License-No-Work Gate

Where a license, certification, registration, permit or professional authorization is required:

> **No license, no regulated work.**

Aurex should not perform regulated work first and regularize it later.

---

## 81. No-Authority-No-Control Gate

Where asset control, system access, data processing or infrastructure connection requires authorization:

> **No authority, no control.**

Technical feasibility is not permission.

---

## 82. Minimum Legal Baseline

Before material external activity, Aurex should know:

```text
WHO ARE WE LEGALLY?
WHO MAY COMMIT US?
WHAT CAN WE LAWFULLY DO NOW?
WHAT REQUIRES APPROVAL?
WHAT REQUIRES A LICENSE?
WHAT REQUIRES A CONTRACT?
WHAT REQUIRES OWNER AUTHORITY?
WHAT REQUIRES PROFESSIONAL CERTIFICATION?
WHAT MUST WE NOT CLAIM YET?
```

---

## 83. Legal Readiness Gate

The minimum legal readiness gate should confirm:

- current legal identity is truthful;
- entity path is either selected with advice or intentionally still under review;
- authority to sign and commit is documented;
- IP treatment is understood;
- data roles and obligations are identified;
- energy-regulatory boundaries are mapped;
- prohibited claims are controlled;
- evidence gaps are recorded.

Passing this gate does not mean all future activity is authorized.

---

## 84. Pilot Gate

A real-world pilot should not proceed unless the pilot-specific legal gate confirms:

- legal contracting party;
- signed pilot agreement where appropriate;
- site permission;
- asset-owner permission;
- control authority;
- data rights;
- electrical professional requirements;
- safety requirements;
- insurance requirements;
- regulator review or authorization if required;
- truthful public claims.

---

## 85. National Programme Gate

A national-scale programme requires materially stronger authority than a software prototype or research programme.

Before representing any national programme as official, Aurex should require evidence of:

- lawful institutional role;
- government or statutory authority where required;
- utility/operator arrangements;
- regulator position where required;
- public procurement or funding basis where relevant;
- data governance;
- security governance;
- liability allocation;
- public accountability.

---

## 86. Institutional Readiness Maturity

Aurex's legal and institutional readiness may mature through:

```text
L0 - UNFORMED
Conceptual institution; legal identity unresolved.

L1 - READINESS MAPPED
Legal questions, authority gaps and evidence needs identified.

L2 - ENTITY FORMED
Legal entity established and basic records created.

L3 - OPERATING BASELINE
Contracts, tax, data, IP, safety and financial controls fit current activities.

L4 - REGULATED-ACTIVITY READY
Required licenses, professional authority, regulator positions and operational controls in place for specific regulated activities.

L5 - MULTI-JURISDICTIONAL / NATIONAL-ROLE READY
Institutional arrangements, legal authority, governance and assurance are mature enough for cross-border or national-scale roles.
```

## 87. Current-Maturity Truthfulness

Based only on this documentation set, Aurex should not claim maturity beyond:

```text
L1 - READINESS MAPPED
```

unless independent repository evidence establishes incorporation, legal authority, contracts, licenses, tax status, insurance, procurement eligibility, field-pilot authority or regulatory approval.

The maturity label should rise only when evidence rises.
