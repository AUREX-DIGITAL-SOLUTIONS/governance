Task 21 does something different: it does not add another major governance domain. It brings the existing domains together into **one coherent institutional system of record**.

> **Documentation becomes institutional infrastructure only when the documents form one coherent system of truth.**

---

## 1. Purpose

The Organization Documentation Baseline establishes the first complete documented institutional architecture for Aurex Digital Solutions.

Its purpose is to ensure that Aurex can answer, consistently:

- What is Aurex?
- Why does it exist?
- What problem is it addressing?
- What does it believe?
- How is authority governed?
- How are decisions made?
- How is technology developed?
- How is risk managed?
- How does work happen?
- How does the institution intend to grow?
- How is it financed?
- How does it collaborate?
- How does it conduct research?
- How are programmes governed?
- What legal boundaries apply?
- What is Aurex actually capable of today?
- What remains proposed or unvalidated?
- What happens next?

The answer should no longer depend primarily on founder memory.

## 3. What Baseline v1.0 Means

Baseline v1.0 means:

> Aurex has established its first internally coherent documented institutional architecture from which future changes can be deliberately governed.

It means a reference point exists.

It does **not** mean:

- institutional completion;
- legal incorporation;
- formal adoption of every policy;
- regulatory authority;
- government approval;
- validated National DERMS;
- production readiness;
- completed organization building;
- utility authority;
- electrical contracting authority;
- market authority;
- evidence that a national platform has been deployed.

Version 1.0 records the current documented state. It does not finish the institution.

---

## 4. Baseline vs Completion

```text
BASELINE
   ≠
COMPLETION
```

A baseline answers:

> **What is the current documented state?**

Future versions answer:

> **What changed, why did it change, and under whose authority?**

The distinction matters because documentation can create discipline, but it cannot create external facts by itself.

> **Aurex Digital Solutions**

## 18. Last-Mile Visibility Gap Baseline

The phrase **last-mile visibility gap** should remain identified as an **Aurex research framing unless independently validated as established external terminology in the relevant context**.

Aurex may investigate whether meaningful last-mile visibility, interoperability or coordination gaps exist across Kenyan facility and distributed-energy contexts. It should not state that Kenya officially recognizes a problem under that exact term unless evidence supports the claim.

The Kenya last-mile distinction should remain precise:

```text
GENERAL ACCESS / CONNECTIVITY / ELECTRIFICATION ISSUES
        ≠
AUREX LAST-MILE VISIBILITY GAP RESEARCH FRAMING
```

Aurex should also acknowledge existing digital systems rather than implying that Kenya has no digital energy, utility, metering, grid, customer, telemetry or operational systems. The research question is about what is visible, to whom, at what resolution, under what authority, through which systems, and with what operational consequence.

## 20. Research Baseline

Research is the discipline for reducing uncertainty.

The immediate research question is:

> Where do meaningful visibility, interoperability and coordination gaps actually exist across Kenyan facility-level and distributed-energy contexts, and what evidence shows that solving them would produce useful operational, economic, reliability or planning outcomes?

Research must preserve the difference between facts, observations, inferences, hypotheses, assumptions, estimates, simulations, experiments and vision.

---

## 21. Engineering Baseline

Engineering should follow validated need.

The immediate Facility EMS engineering objective is:

> Build a controlled facility-level research/prototype environment only after the target problem, users, data boundary, safety boundary, authority boundary and evidence gate are sufficiently defined.

The evidence gate should answer:

```text
WHAT PROBLEM IS BEING TESTED?
WHO EXPERIENCES IT?
WHAT DATA IS NEEDED?
WHO MAY AUTHORIZE ACCESS?
WHAT SAFETY BOUNDARIES APPLY?
WHAT RESULT WOULD VALIDATE OR REFUTE THE HYPOTHESIS?
```

## 22. Safety Baseline

Safety applies before optimization.

Aurex should not connect to, control, influence or represent operational energy infrastructure without suitable technical controls, legal authority, site permission, competent personnel and risk review.

---

## 23. Security Baseline

Security is part of institutional legitimacy.

Documentation should distinguish research systems, prototypes, pilots and production systems. Claims of secure operation must be proportionate to actual testing, threat modeling, controls, monitoring, incident response and independent assurance.

---

## 24. Open-Source and Upstream Baseline

Aurex may use and contribute to open-source technology, but open-source use does not imply ownership, partnership, endorsement or permission to use upstream names beyond license terms.

Aurex should respect upstream licenses, attribution, notices, trademarks, contribution rules and security-disclosure expectations.

---

## 25. Data Baseline

Data rights must be explicit.

Aurex should not imply it has rights to energy telemetry, personal data, facility records, utility data, government datasets or third-party operational data without evidence of lawful access, permission, license, public availability or another valid basis.

Energy data is not automatically personal data, but it may become personal or sensitive depending on identifiability, context, granularity and use.

---

## 26. AI and Human Accountability Baseline

AI may support research, documentation, engineering, analysis and operations.

AI output should not replace human accountability for safety, legal interpretation, public claims, partner commitments, infrastructure control, security-sensitive decisions or evidence classification.

---

   ≠

## 44. Records vs Policies

A policy states intended rules or governance principles.

A record documents that something happened.

```text
POLICY
   ≠
RECORD
```

Do not create fictional records to make the institution appear more mature. If a meeting, adoption, approval, partnership, payment, experiment, pilot or legal filing has not happened, the documentation should not imply that it has.
